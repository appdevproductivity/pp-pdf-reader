<section class="pp-touch-config">
  <article>
    <h2>Configurable actions</h2>
    <p>Every touch region can be assigned the action that best fits your reading workflow.</p>
  </article>
  <article>
    <h2>Three gestures per region</h2>
    <p>Each region accepts tap, long press, and double tap, with one action configured for each gesture.</p>
  </article>
  <article>
    <h2>Adjustable region size</h2>
    <p>Region sizes are configurable, so the page edges can match your grip, hand size, and reading posture.</p>
  </article>
  <article>
    <h2>Invisible while reading</h2>
    <p>Touch regions are transparent during normal use. Show them only when configuring region sizes.</p>
  </article>
</section>
